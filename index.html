00·D 规则可读性与反欺诈焊接RULE READABILITY & ANTI-FRAUD WELDING

潜规则的对立面不是道德说教，而是明规则 + 反欺诈机制

The opposite of hidden rules is not moral preaching, but public rules + anti-fraud mechanisms

《自我学》V1.3 确立了一条硬原则：撒谎不是主权的行使。感受主权不保护蓄意伪造的感受陈述。HPRP 继承这条原则，并将其转化为机构设计层的要求——不可读的规则是欺诈的温床；没有反欺诈机制的公共系统，会让撒谎者用主权语言压制受害者。因此，规则可读性与反欺诈机制必须被焊接在一起。

Self Studies V1.3 established a hard principle: lying is not the exercise of sovereignty. Feeling sovereignty does not protect deliberately fabricated feeling-statements. HPRP inherits this principle and translates it into institutional design requirements — unreadable rules are breeding grounds for fraud; public systems without anti-fraud mechanisms allow liars to use sovereignty language to suppress victims. Therefore, rule readability and anti-fraud mechanisms must be welded together.

焊接原则 · 规则可读性 x 反欺诈
WELDING PRINCIPLE · RULE READABILITY × ANTI-FRAUD

明规则本身就是反欺诈的第一道防线。

Public rules themselves are the first line of anti-fraud defense.

不可读的规则制造依附、求人、潜规则和羞辱——也制造撒谎的机会。当规则清晰、路径明确、失败后仍可再次进入时，一个人不需要通过撒谎来获得通行。当撒谎发生，明规则系统必须有能力依据独立可核验的事实进行仲裁，而不是依赖地位、关系或权力。

Unreadable rules produce dependency, begging, hidden rules, and humiliation — and they also create opportunities for lying. When rules are clear, pathways are explicit, and re-entry after failure is possible, a person does not need to lie to gain passage. When lying does occur, the public-rule system must be capable of arbitration based on independently verifiable facts — not on status, connections, or power.

因此，HPRP 要求每一个工具、协议、制度安放节点都必须同时满足两个条件：① 普通人读得懂入口和路径；② 系统内置了基于事实基础而非地位的反欺诈校验机制。

Therefore, HPRP requires that every tool, protocol, and institutional placement node must simultaneously satisfy two conditions: ① ordinary people can read the entry points and pathways; ② the system has built-in anti-fraud verification based on factual basis, not on status.

反欺诈机制设计清单
ANTI-FRAUD MECHANISM DESIGN CHECKLIST
F1 · 事实基础
所有涉及资源分配或权利判定的声称，必须可被独立核验。通话记录、监控、平台数据、多方证词——不可仅凭单方陈述做出对他人的惩罚性裁决。
All claims involving resource allocation or rights determination must be independently verifiable. Call records, surveillance, platform data, multiple witness accounts — punitive judgments against others cannot be based solely on one-sided statements.
F2 · 结构位置校正
事实基础相当或难以判定时，优先保护结构中更弱势的一方。权力不对等会影响一个人捍卫自身主权的能力，仲裁必须补偿性校正。
When factual bases are comparable, prioritize the structurally more vulnerable party. Power asymmetries affect one's ability to defend sovereignty; arbitration must apply compensatory correction.
F3 · 恢复优先
反欺诈机制的目标是恢复被侵犯的主权，不是惩罚。恢复后，系统应保留返回路径，不将被误判或已纠正的人永久标记。
The goal of anti-fraud mechanisms is to restore violated sovereignty, not to punish. After restoration, the system should preserve return pathways and not permanently mark those who were wrongly judged or have corrected themselves.
不可读的规则是欺诈的土壤。明规则 + 基于事实的仲裁 = 规则可读性与反欺诈的焊接点。
Unreadable rules are the soil of fraud. Public rules + fact-based arbitration = the weld point between rule readability and anti-fraud.
00·E 主权冲突仲裁机构化SOVEREIGNTY CONFLICT ARBITRATION INSTITUTIONALIZATION

当两个主权声称碰撞，制度必须能够裁决而不制造新的废墟

When two sovereignty claims collide, institutions must be able to adjudicate without creating new ruins

《自我学》V1.3 建立了主权冲突仲裁原则：优先保护有独立可核验事实基础且处于结构中更弱势一方的主权。HPRP 将这一原则机构化——不是作为道德呼吁，而是作为制度安放层的设计要求。任何一个接住人的公共系统，都必须预装这一仲裁机制。

Self Studies V1.3 established the sovereignty conflict arbitration principle: when claims collide, prioritize the claim with independently verifiable factual basis and belonging to the structurally more vulnerable party. HPRP institutionalizes this principle — not as a moral appeal, but as a design requirement for the institutional placement layer. Any public system that holds people must pre-install this arbitration mechanism.

A01 · ARBITRATION ENTRY

仲裁入口

Arbitration Entry

每一个 HPRP 协议节点（庇护、身份恢复、健康、收入、关系、尊严）必须预留一个“冲突报告与仲裁”入口。当服务接受者与提供者之间、接受者与接受者之间发生主权声称冲突时，该入口启动，而非直接升级为惩罚或驱逐。

Every HPRP protocol node (shelter, identity, health, income, relational, dignity) must reserve a "conflict report and arbitration" entry. When sovereignty claims collide between service recipients and providers, or among recipients, the entry activates — not escalating directly to punishment or expulsion.

A02 · DUAL CRITERIA

双重判准

Dual Criteria

仲裁者必须同时评估：① 事实基础——是否有独立于声称者的可核验证据；② 结构位置——双方在权力、信息、资源上的不对等程度。两项判准必须同时被记录和公开，不得以“综合判断”模糊处理。

Arbitrators must simultaneously assess: ① factual basis — is there independently verifiable evidence; ② structural position — the degree of asymmetry in power, information, and resources between the parties. Both criteria must be recorded and disclosed; "comprehensive judgment" without transparency is prohibited.

A03 · RESTORATION TRACK

恢复轨道

Restoration Track

仲裁结果不是惩罚，而是恢复。被侵犯的主权需要被恢复（如撤销错误扣罚、道歉、赔偿、制度漏洞修补）。侵犯方若愿意纠正，应保留返回路径，不被永久放逐。但明知故犯的欺诈行为需承担相应后果，且后果必须预先明文规定。

The outcome of arbitration is not punishment, but restoration. Violated sovereignty must be restored (e.g., reversing wrongful penalties, apology, compensation, fixing institutional loopholes). If the violator is willing to correct, return pathways should be preserved, not permanent exile. But deliberate fraud carries specified consequences, which must be publicly stated in advance.

A04 · NO RETALIATION

禁止报复

No Retaliation

任何提起仲裁的人，不得因此被降低服务质量、取消资格或遭受隐性惩罚。制度必须将“提起仲裁”与“服务资格”完全解耦。报复行为本身成为新的仲裁案件。

Anyone who initiates arbitration must not face reduced service quality, disqualification, or hidden punishment as a result. Institutions must fully decouple "initiating arbitration" from "service eligibility." Retaliation itself becomes a new arbitration case.

Before return, know which layer is bearing weight

维度01
DIM01

Bodily Safety: Can I sleep tonight in a place free from violation? Are my pain, hunger, and illness being seen and addressed?
维度02
DIM02
感受被承认度：我的情绪和痛苦是否被认真对待，而不是被解释为“太敏感”、“不懂规矩”或“精神问题”？
Feeling Recognition: Are my emotions and suffering taken seriously, not dismissed as "too sensitive," "don't know the rules," or "mental issues"?
维度03
DIM03
节律完整度：我的睡眠、休息、进食、专注的节律是否被打散？我是否被迫用别人的速度生活？
Rhythm Integrity: Is my sleep, rest, eating, and focus rhythm shattered? Am I forced to live at someone else's pace?
维度04
DIM04
感质可见度：我作为独特个体被看见，还是被当作一个类别、一个号码、一个需要处理的案件？
Qualia Visibility: Am I seen as a unique person, or as a category, a number, a case to be processed?
维度05
DIM05
规则可读度：我知道下一步该做什么吗？我遇到问题时知道该问谁、不会被羞辱吗？
Rule Readability: Do I know what to do next? When I encounter a problem, do I know who to ask without being humiliated?
维度06
DIM06
认知开放度：我是否能接触到不同阶层、不同处境的人的真实生活，还是我的信息基础被封闭在一个圈层内？
Cognitive Openness: Can I access the real lives of people in different classes and situations, or is my information basis sealed within a closed circle?
主权容量自评使用规则
SOVEREIGNTY CAPACITY SELF-ASSESSMENT RULE

此自评表不是诊断工具，不用于决定一个人是否有资格获得帮助。它用于帮助个人和外展工作者快速定位“哪一层首先需要被接住”。每一层的回答只有使用者自己有权解释。系统不得以“主权容量不足”为由拒绝提供服务——恰恰相反，低容量意味着更需要被接住。

This self-assessment is not a diagnostic tool and is not used to determine eligibility for help. It helps individuals and outreach workers quickly locate "which layer needs to be held first." Only the user has the right to interpret their own answers. The system must not deny services on grounds of "low sovereignty capacity" — on the contrary, low capacity means greater need to be held.

仲裁不是惩罚，是恢复。制度必须既能裁决，又不制造新的废墟。主权容量评估则是恢复前的定位仪。
Arbitration is not punishment; it is restoration. Institutions must be able to adjudicate without creating new ruins. Sovereignty capacity assessment is the locator before restoration.
